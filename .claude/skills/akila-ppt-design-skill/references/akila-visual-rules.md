# Akila Visual Rules

## Typography

- English large titles: `Clarity City` with bold styling.
- English body copy: `Clarity City` regular.
- Chinese titles: `OPPOSans B`.
- Chinese body copy: `OPPOSans R`.
- PowerPoint stores font family and style separately. In OOXML terms, use `latin=Clarity City` with bold enabled for title-like text; use `latin=Clarity City` regular for body text; use `ea/cs=OPPOSans B` for title-like text; use `ea/cs=OPPOSans R` for body text.

## Color System

- `#1E2856`: primary dark blue. Use as the main structural anchor, title color on light surfaces, dark surface, footer, or major brand field.
- `#385AEA`: primary blue and accent color. Use for emphasis, key labels, outlines, highlights, selected data accents, and controlled UI-like indicators. Do not use it as a dominant large-area fill by default.
- `#B523B9`: controlled accent for unusually dense content when extra category or state differentiation materially improves comprehension. Keep total visual proportion below 5% of the slide.
- `#45C5CC`: controlled accent for large titles on `#1E2856` dark-blue surfaces.
- Neutral colors: use for body copy, small descriptions, captions, and long-form text. Body copy and small text must not use accent colors.

## Color Constraints

- Do not create pages that rely only on `#45C5CC` and `#385AEA`; the result feels too cold.
- Do not add `#B523B9` just because a slide is dense. Use it only when content has real categories, stages, states, or comparison groups that need separation.
- If a slide becomes visually noisy, reduce accent usage before changing the layout.
- Accent colors must support comprehension, not decoration.

## Akila Visual Language

- Akila should read as a real-building digital twin: physical space, data platform, AI operation, predictive maintenance, energy optimization, facilities intelligence, and operating evidence.
- Prefer clear information surfaces, structured grids, spatial or system evidence, and controlled data-led overlays.
- Avoid abstract AI-brain visuals, cyberpunk neon, random dashboard widgets, generic SaaS screens, and purely decorative technology effects.
- Use a few large semantic zones rather than many small UI-like cards when explaining business or platform logic.

## Layout Behavior

- Start from the content purpose, not from the most visually attractive slide.
- Use the template for visual language and proven compositions, but adapt density, grouping, and emphasis to the actual content.
- Keep title hierarchy strong and readable.
- Avoid text clipping, orphan final words, cramped card padding, and unmanaged edge proximity.
- For diagrams, use clear node hierarchy and connector logic. Connectors should express actual relationships, not simply mirror a previous slide.

## Chart And Diagram Adaptation

- Chart-like and diagram-like slides must be revised based on the actual dataset, relationship logic, comparisons, content grouping, and message hierarchy.
- `Reference only` slides are approved visual references whose structure is tied to a specific purpose.
- If the real content requires a different comparison, flow, node relationship, sequence, or evidence structure, rebuild the diagram or chart structure first.
